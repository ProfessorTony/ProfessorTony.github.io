💻 Course Description: Your Mission, Should You Choose to Accept It...

Welcome to CSN 40: Information Security Fundamentals! I am absolutely thrilled to have you in class this semester as we dive into one of the most dynamic, fast-paced, and crucial fields in technology today. Step onto the frontlines of our connected age, where safeguarding critical data is the ultimate mission in modern technology. This course empowers you to decode the tactics of cyber adversaries, master essential security architectures like firewalls and cryptography, and develop rock-solid incident response strategies. By merging hands-on technical defense with sharp strategic planning, you will transform from an everyday technology user into a formidable guardian of our digital world. I am here to support your success every step of the way. Get ready for an exciting journey into the heart of cybersecurity.

Official Course Description:

      This course is an introduction to the various technical and administrative aspects of Information Security and Assurance. This course provides the foundation for understanding the key issues associated with protecting information assets, determining the levels of protection and response to security incidents, and designing a consistent, reasonable information security system, with appropriate intrusion detection and reporting features.Students will be exposed to the spectrum of security activities, methods, methodologies, and procedures. Coverage will include inspection and protection of information assets, detection of and reaction to threats to information assets, and examination of pre- and post-incident procedures,technical and managerial responses and an overview of the Information Security Planning and Staffing functions. (CSU)

🎯 Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • Identify and prioritize information assets.
  • Identify and prioritize threats to information assets.
  • Define an information security strategy and architecture.
  • Plan for and respond to intruders in an information system. 
  • Describe legal and public relations implications of security and privacy issues.   
  • Present a disaster recovery plan for recovery of information assets after an incident.

💡 Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • ** Subject matter knowledge will be measured by demonstrating fundamental knowledge of Management Information Systems (M.I.S.) and its terminology. **
  • Students will demonstrate understanding concept of Information Security and Assurances by answering essay questions in assignments, projects and exams.
  • Students will demonstrate understanding how to secure information in a computer by configuring either a virtual computer, or a computer in the lab to withstand virtual and simulated attacks.

🪑“Excessive Absenteeism” Policy!

“Excessive absenteeism” in an online course is defined as not participating in the course for an adequate amount of time to successfully pass the course or adequately complete the course. Participation in the course involves completing course activities – simply logging in does not count as participation. If a student does not submit assignments during a time period of 8 consecutive days, misses 2 or more assignments, OR if a student does not complete a minimum of 65% of assignments by the drop date, the student runs the risk of being dropped from the course for excessive absenteeism. Pro Tip: Consistency is your most effective tool for success.
