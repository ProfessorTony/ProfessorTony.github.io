💻 Course Description: Your Mission, Should You Choose to Accept It...

Welcome to CSW 38: I am thrilled to lead you on this creative journey into the world of front-end computer graphics. Web animation is much more than moving shapes on a screen—it is the dynamic intersection of logic, mathematics, physics, and visual art. In modern web engineering, the ability to generate high-performance visual experiences, interactive dashboards, online games, and rich data visualizations is a powerful superpower. 

Official Course Description:

      This course is designed to provide the student with an understanding of incorporating animation, sound,graphics, and interactivity into Web pages using the HTML Canvas tag. The student explores the ins and outs of Canvas' capabilities to create and manipulate web page animation and interactivity using Javascript. (HBCU, CSU)

🎯 Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • Understand the HTML DOM model. 
  • Create and work with the HTML Canvas element. 
  • Use Canvas's capability to draw and animate images. 
  • Understand and apply Web safe color issues.
  • Use the HTML5 Canvas Text API.  6.
  • Load and use external images into HTML5 Canvas. 
  • Be able to mix Video with HTML5 Canvas. 
  • Use math and physics with animation.   
  • Incorporate and control sound in web animation. 
  • Apply interactivity with actions and event handlers.

💡 Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • ** Subject matter knowledge will be measured by demonstrating fundamental knowledge of Management Information Systems (M.I.S.) and its terminology. **
  • Design and produce visually attractive, usable, and accessible Web content using the HTML5 Canvas element.

🪑“Excessive Absenteeism” Policy!

“Excessive absenteeism” in an online course is defined as not participating in the course for an adequate amount of time to successfully pass the course or adequately complete the course. Participation in the course involves completing course activities – simply logging in does not count as participation. If a student does not submit assignments during a time period of 8 consecutive days, misses 2 or more assignments, OR if a student does not complete a minimum of 65% of assignments by the drop date, the student runs the risk of being dropped from the course for excessive absenteeism. Pro Tip: Consistency is your most effective tool for success.

⏰Late Work: Beat the Clock!

Assignments have due dates for a reason – they help you stay on track and build knowledge sequentially. Assignments submitted late will have a penalty.
