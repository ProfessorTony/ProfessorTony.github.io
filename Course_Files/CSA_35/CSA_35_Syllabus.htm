💻 Course Description: Your Mission, Should You Choose to Accept It...

Welcome to CSA 35: Multimedia Presentations, where you will master the art of designing dynamic and impactful professional slide decks! This course goes beyond basic text, diving into the integration of graphics, animations, video, and audio to create presentations that truly captivate an audience. Get ready to elevate your communication skills by exploring advanced Microsoft Office features, cutting-edge AI tools, and professional layout techniques that will set you apart in the business world.

Official Course Description:

     This course is designed to introduce concepts required for creating business presentations with text,graphics, and multimedia capabilities. Presentation layout and design techniques are emphasized. (HBCU,CSU)

🎯 Course Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • Identify the components of a presentation document.
  • Use editing, formatting, slide show, and printing functions.
  • Apply presentation software tools to create a slide show, notes, and handouts.
  • Design appropriate presentations that are based on the audience's viewpoint, the environmental considerations, and the client's goals and objectives.
  • Select appropriate graphic images that convey the information instead of text only slides.
  • Choose the appropriate type, color, and design template for a slide presentation.
  • Evaluate and apply appropriate multimedia to enhance presentations.
  • Identify and apply industry-specific employability skills.

💡 Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  • ** Subject matter knowledge will be measured by demonstrating fundamental knowledge of Multimedia Presentation and terminology. **
  • Use the tools of presentation software to produce a presentation for a specific purpose and audience.
  • Identify the design elements of a slide presentation developed using good presentation design elements.

🪑“Excessive Absenteeism” Policy!

“Excessive absenteeism” in an online course is defined as not participating in the course for an adequate amount of time to successfully pass the course or adequately complete the course. Participation in the course involves completing course activities – simply logging in does not count as participation. If a student does not submit assignments during a time period of 8 consecutive days, missing 2 assignments or more, OR if a student does not complete a minimum of 65% of assignments by the drop date, the student runs the risk of being dropped from the course for excessive absenteeism. Pro Tip: Consistency is your most effective tool for success.

⏰Late Work: Beat the Clock!

Assignments have due dates for a reason – they help you stay on track and build knowledge sequentially. Assignments submitted late will have a penalty. Again, you are responsible for doing the work and doing it on time! Late penalty will be up to 10% of assignment score. (Depending on number of days late). Pro Tip: Aim to finish early! You'll thank yourself later.

🛡️Academic Integrity: Code with Honor!

This is a big one! All work submitted in this course must be **your own original work**, unless explicitly stated otherwise. **What's Okay:** Discussing concepts, getting help understanding errors. **What's NOT Okay:** Copying written work or code, sharing completed assignments, having someone else do your work. Any instance of academic dishonesty will result in a **zero for the assignment/exam**, and may be reported to the college. Let's keep it honest! Tools such as Turnitin may be used to check for similarities.
